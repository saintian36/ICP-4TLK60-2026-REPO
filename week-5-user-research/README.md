# Week 5 – User Research & Usability Testing

## Objective
To evaluate how easily users could complete the core food-ordering journey using the interactive prototype.

## Testing Method
Moderated usability testing sessions were conducted using the Figma prototype. Participants were asked to complete realistic ordering tasks while thinking out loud.

## Participants
5 participants aged 20–30 who regularly use food delivery applications.

## Tasks
1. Find a restaurant and open its menu.
2. Add one food item to the cart.
3. Review the cart and proceed to checkout.
4. Complete the order.

## Key Findings
- Some users hesitated before locating the cart button.
- One participant expected a confirmation message after adding items.
- All participants completed checkout successfully.
- Pricing breakdown was clear and understandable.

## Improvements Made
- Increased visibility of cart indicator.
- Strengthened emphasis on primary call-to-action buttons.
- Improved spacing and clarity in checkout summary.

## Conclusion
Usability testing validated the clarity of the core ordering experience while identifying minor friction points that were refined for improved usability.
