# Week 6 – Uber Eats Checkout Redesign

## Overview
This project redesigns the Uber Eats checkout experience to improve clarity, reduce cognitive load, and strengthen the final purchase flow.

The redesign focuses on simplifying pricing information, reducing visual clutter, and improving the hierarchy of the primary action.


## Problem

The current Uber Eats checkout presents a high density of information including promotions, multiple fees, and several competing sections.

This creates friction at the final decision stage and may reduce clarity around the total price and the primary action.

Key issues identified:

- Too much information visible at once
- Promotional sections competing with checkout content
- Pricing breakdown difficult to scan quickly
- Primary CTA ("Place Order") not visually dominant


## Redesign Goals

- Reduce cognitive load during checkout
- Improve pricing transparency
- Simplify visual hierarchy
- Strengthen the primary CTA
- Create a calmer and clearer checkout layout

---

## Low-Fidelity Redesign

The checkout structure was simplified using clearer information grouping and progressive disclosure.

New structure:

Delivery Address  
Order Summary 
Grouped Pricing Breakdown  
Payment Method  
Primary CTA


## Key Improvements

- Reduced visual clutter by collapsing non-essential sections
- Grouped multiple fees into clearer pricing categories
- Improved visibility of the total price
- Strengthened CTA placement and hierarchy


## Expected Impact

- Faster checkout scanning
- Reduced user hesitation
- Increased clarity around pricing
- Stronger conversion flow
